## 9. What to say in the review

Phase 2 scored 21 different models on 7 drivers, every model on the same expanding-window folds and hold-out as the Phase 1 baselines. A model beat seasonal naive on mean CV MASE for 7 of 7 drivers. The largest gain is on eci_wages_quarterly: arima reaches CV MASE 0.328 against 1.737 for seasonal naive (81% lower). For burst_tokens_total_daily the chosen model is ensemble (ensemble), CV MASE 0.424 and hold-out MASE 1.003. For burst_requests_hourly the chosen model is ensemble (ensemble), CV MASE 0.755 and hold-out MASE 1.834. Hold-out MASE is higher than CV MASE for 7 of 7 chosen models, so the final two weeks (or last periods) are harder than the backtest windows; the hold-out was never used for choosing. The chosen models come from these families: arima, baseline, ensemble; no single family wins everywhere. Empirical leave-one-fold-out intervals are too narrow for 71 of 118 model-driver pairs; with as few as 4 folds each interval rests on 3 errors per step. The day-77 outage check is FAIL (|robust z| 1.752 against 3.5). Hyper-parameters were chosen on the first training window only and kept fixed, so no fold ever saw its own test data.

## 10. Limitations

- Short window: BurstGPT has 87 clean days (12 weeks); burst_tokens_total_daily, burst_tokens_api_daily, burst_tokens_chat_daily have fewer than 5 folds, so mean CV MASE rests on few windows.
- Hyper-parameters (ARIMA orders, moving-average window, Ridge alpha) are tuned on the first training window only; smoothing parameters are re-estimated in every fold.
- Intervals are empirical (leave-one-fold-out quantiles of absolute errors); with few folds the 95% quantile is close to the largest of a handful of errors and under-covers.
- The ensemble's two components are picked by the same CV MASE it is then scored on, so its CV score is optimistic; its hold-out score is the fair check.
- BurstGPT weekend (day_of_cycle 4 and 5) is inferred from lower volume, not known.
- Prophet would use an arbitrary start date (2000-01-03) as an index only.
- burst_requests_hourly: the 8 capped folds (Phase 1 settings) test positions 336-863 of the 1920 pre-hold-out hours, not the most recent weeks.
- All models forecast the raw scale (no log transform), and forecasts are clipped at 0.

## 11. Deviations from the Phase 2 prompt

- Day-77 check: day 77 lies inside the BurstGPT hold-out (days 73-86), so the anomaly screen uses the chosen model refit on the full window (days 0-86); the other residual diagnostics use the pre-hold-out fit as specified.
- The requests feature is lagged 14 days, not 7: the hold-out (and Phase 3) horizon is 14 days, and a 7-day lag would need future requests there.
- Machine-learning models also run for wildchat_conversations_daily (a daily series with more observations than BurstGPT); its is_weekend is the real calendar Saturday/Sunday.
- SARIMAX for daily series uses is_weekend (also for WildChat daily); for quarterly and monthly series no exogenous variable is defined, so SARIMAX is skipped there.
- SARIMAX uses D=0 instead of the handoff D=1 for burst_tokens_chat_daily, burst_requests_hourly, wildchat_conversations_daily: is_weekend is a period-7 dummy, so seasonal differencing at 7 would wipe it out.
- Hourly SARIMAX uses Fourier terms (K=3 for 24 and 168) with D=0: the deterministic terms replace the seasonal difference D=1 from the handoff.
- ARIMA-family forecasts that are non-finite or move more than 5x the training range (an explosive unconstrained AR part) are treated as failed fits and that fold falls back to ARIMA(1, d, 0); the fallbacks are listed in params.csv and in the leaderboard flags.
- Trend term in the ARIMA family: constant when d + D = 0, drift when only d = 1, no trend term otherwise.
- Every forecast is clipped at 0 because all modelled series are non-negative.
- The moving-average window is chosen by the MAE of a flat forecast on the last 20% of the first training window; Ridge alpha by one-step MAE on the same share.
- Over-differencing flag from Phase 1 (ECI, PPI): d=2 is not refit; the comparison of D=0 versus D=1 is made by CV MASE of ARIMA (D=0) against SARIMA (D=1), because AICc is not comparable across differencing orders.
- Prophet is filed under the family 'deep' (the optional-challenger family) since the prompt gives it no family.
- Each model has a wall-clock budget of 240 s per driver, checked between fits.
- Hold-out intervals for steps beyond the CV horizon reuse the quantile of the last CV step.
- ARIMA parameter tables for every ARIMA-family fit are written to an extra table, arima_params.csv.
- The LSTM is off by default and did not run (use --with-lstm).
