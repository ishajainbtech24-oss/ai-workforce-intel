# UI critique of the Phase 5 dashboard ("before" screenshots)

Screenshots: `before/<page>_1440.png` and `before/<page>_390.png`, taken with Playwright (Chromium) on 2026-11-16.
Each point below was fixed in the polish pass; the "after" column says how.

| # | Where | What looks plain, cramped, inconsistent, flat or dull | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | All pages | Two type families fight each other: Plus Jakarta headings, but body, labels, nav and charts fall back to Streamlit's own Source Sans because the Inter rule never reaches Streamlit's widgets. | One family (Inter) for everything, Plus Jakarta only for display headlines; tabular figures on all numbers. |
| 2 | All pages | Flat paper background with two faint blobs; no texture or depth, so the glass cards have nothing to sit on. | Slow aurora backdrop, faint dot grid, two blurred accent orbs behind the content. |
| 3 | Top bar | Navigation is Streamlit's default: grey chip for the active page, no glass, and the product name sits in the page body, detached from it. | Sticky translucent bar with blur; an accent pill that glides to the active page; product name and "Example company" pill in a matching header row. |
| 4 | Phone (390 px) | Navigation collapses to a bare "»" icon with no label; a first-time reader cannot tell it is the page menu. | Labelled "Pages" button styled as a pill. |
| 5 | Overview hero | The 3D shape is a muddy grey-blue low-poly blob with one stray highlight; it reads as a rendering glitch, not glass. | Glass torus knot with rim light and clearcoat, depth particles with small cursor parallax, faint grid floor; text keeps a clear area. |
| 6 | Overview cards | All four cards look identical: no icon, no status, and the "Pays for itself" sparkline repeats the "Running balance" one. The "Expected outlook" caption floats 40 px away from the cards. | Icon per card, status chip from the real data ("Needs attention" / "On track"), sparkline that draws itself, cursor-following light; caption joined to the cards as a section label; payback card now shows each month's benefit minus cost against a zero line instead of a duplicate. |
| 7 | All charts | Axis labels read "$-200k" (minus after the dollar sign, lower-case k) while the cards say "-$96.6K"; chart titles sit flush against the card edge. | Money ticks use the same formatter as the cards ("-$200K"); chart cards get proper padding and a consistent title style. |
| 8 | Overview chart | Dots on every month clutter the lines; no outlook stands out; the lowest point and the pays-for-itself month are not called out. | Smooth lines without dots, the expected outlook (the one the cards show) highlighted and the others dimmed, annotations for its lowest point and for the month the optimistic outlook pays for itself; the line draws itself on load. |
| 9 | Costs | Hard-edged, heavily saturated stacked bars; the donut is flat and its legend repeats the tags above. "Cost" bars on the benefit chart are a dead grey. | Rounded bars with a softer palette; 3D donut with lifting slices (flat view still available); cost bars in a tinted neutral that works in both themes. |
| 10 | What matters most | The page heading and the chart title are the same sentence, one under the other. Amber vs indigo reads as "bad vs good" when it only means lower vs higher. | Chart title says what the bars mean ("If each choice were 20% lower or higher"); added a ranked depth-bar view of how far each choice can move the result. |
| 11 | When it pays for itself | The comparison table is drawn on a canvas (cannot follow the theme, cannot be searched or read by a screen reader) and looks like a spreadsheet. | Styled HTML table with an outlook colour dot per row. |
| 12 | Try your own numbers | "Reset to example" sits above the form, far from "Update results", and looks like the main action; number boxes show "8.00" for whole hours; the form is one long grey box. | Ghost "Reset" and gradient "Update" buttons in one action row under the form; whole numbers shown without decimals; inputs restyled with an accent focus ring. |
| 13 | Expanders, info and loading states | "How to read this" and "About these numbers" look like bare form fields; loading shows Streamlit's flat grey skeletons. | Glass expanders with an animated chevron; shimmer skeletons; styled info and warning boxes. |
| 14 | Verdict and note boxes | Verdict uses a different, larger font than the page and a 4 px bar that clashes with the 16 px card radius. | Verdict card with an accent gradient edge and the shared type scale. |
| 15 | Whole app | No dark mode; a dark-room or evening reader gets a bright white page. | Light and dark themes, switchable in the top bar and remembered for the session; charts re-coloured for each. |
| 16 | Whole app | Motion is all-or-nothing and only follows the operating-system setting. | "Reduce motion" switch in the top bar (and an `ENABLE_3D` switch in `theme.py`) that swaps every animation and 3D piece for a calm static version. |
| 17 | Footer | Lone grey line; nothing separates it from the content. | Thin gradient rule and a tidier two-part footer line. |
