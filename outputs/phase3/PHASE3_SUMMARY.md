## 3. How the paths are made (residual bootstrap)

- 200 paths per driver. Each path draws ONE backtest fold at random (with replacement) and adds that fold's whole vector of out-of-sample errors (actual minus forecast) to the point forecast, so the way errors hang together across steps is kept.
- Strictly positive series use relative errors: each fold's errors are divided by that fold's level (mean of the last season of values before its origin) and multiplied by the current level (mean of the last season of the window). Series with zeros use the absolute errors.
- Beyond the backtest horizon, step h reuses the drawn fold's last-step error times sqrt(h / H_cv) (growth rule `sqrt`, switch `GROWTH` in src/config.py).
- Every path is clipped at 0. Seed = 42 + the driver's position in the sorted driver names (numpy default_rng), so reruns give identical files.
- Optional, chosen per driver without the final holdout (section 4a): recency weights and level matching.
- Phase 4 sensitivity: `uncertainty_multiplier` is 1 in the saved files; inputs_for_phase4.json recommends re-running Phase 4 with multipliers [1.0, 1.5, 2.0] (path = point + m x (path - point), clipped at 0).

## 8. What to say in the review

Phase 3 turns the chosen model of each of the 7 drivers into 200 simulated future paths plus a point forecast, all written in one ten-column table format. Every chosen model refitted cleanly on the full window. Paths are built by resampling whole vectors of out-of-sample backtest errors, scaled to the current level for the 6 strictly positive series. On the Phase 2 holdout the simulated 80% band covered between 25% and 100% of the actual values; 2 of 7 drivers pass both coverage thresholds. Recency weights and level matching were tested on the last backtest folds only; ppi_hosting_monthly moved from 17% to 33% (80%) and 50% to 83% (95%). For hourly requests the 80% band covered 25% of hours 1 to 24 and 33% of the later hours. The 30-day run-rate of burst_tokens_total_daily has P10 113M, P50 3,160M and P90 6,351M tokens, with the last 16 days extrapolated. Input tokens are 86% of all BurstGPT tokens in the window. The scenario table holds 4 assumption rows taken directly from the METR and FRED tracker data; they are kept apart from the forecasts. Reruns give identical files because every driver has a fixed seed.

## 9. Limitations

- Short window: BurstGPT has 87 clean days; burst_tokens_total_daily, burst_tokens_api_daily, burst_tokens_chat_daily have fewer than 5 backtest folds to resample from.
- BurstGPT has no calendar dates; periods are days or hours after day 86 and the weekend is inferred.
- For dated sources (WildChat, ECI, PPI) `calendar_month` is the month after the data end, which can lie in the past relative to today.
- The bootstrap assumes future errors look like past backtest errors; a regime change is not covered.
- Whole-vector resampling can only reuse the folds that exist: with k folds each step has at most k distinct path values, so the 80% and 95% bands are close to the smallest and largest fold error and often nearly coincide.
- The `sqrt` growth beyond the backtest horizon is an assumption, not an estimate.
- Series with zeros (hourly requests) use absolute errors from early, quieter folds, so their bands can be too narrow when volume is higher; the holdout check measures this.

## 10. Deviations from the Phase 3 prompt

- interval_check.csv has extra columns: `segment` (the hourly driver has rows for all steps and the two horizon splits), `model`, `n_folds_used` and Phase 2's coverage on the same holdout for comparison.
- The run-rate file also holds the point forecast's run-rate as sample_id -1; the extrapolation note is a companion file, burstgpt_monthly_run_rate_NOTE.md, so the CSV stays machine-readable.
- Units: WildChat conversations use the unit `conversations` (from the driver config), not `users`.
- `--fast` does not change any number: it only drops the thin sample paths from the fan charts.
- Ensemble members are clipped at 0 before averaging, as in Phase 2's scoring.
- The scenario table has an extra `kind` column (always `assumption`) so it can never be mixed up with forecasts.
- Validation results are also written to tables/validation.csv.
- Interval options: the pseudo-holdout uses each held-out fold's STORED Phase 2 forecast as the point forecast (no refit), so it is cheap and never reads data after the backtest; identical results keep the options off.
- interval_check.csv keeps coverage_80 / coverage_95 as the AFTER values and adds the before/after columns, miss counts and the data facts used for the causes; the tuning table is tables/interval_tuning.csv.
- The forecast files now use the chosen options, so drivers whose options changed have different paths from the first Phase 3 run.
- cv_residuals.parquet already held ensemble rows, so no ensemble errors had to be rebuilt.
