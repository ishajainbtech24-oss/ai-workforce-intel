## 8. What to say in the review

The engine turns the Phase 3 forecast paths and a company's own inputs into monthly cost, value and break-even over 12 months, with 5,000 Monte Carlo draws per run. All company numbers here belong to an illustrative example company and must be replaced with real ones. In the expected scenario the programme breaks even within 12 months in 2% of draws (median month 12); median total cost $277,628 against median value $177,420. In the optimistic scenario the programme breaks even within 12 months in 74% of draws (median month 10); median total cost $277,628 against median value $332,612. In the downside scenario the programme does not break even within 12 months; median total cost $277,628 against median value $-400,039. The largest cost line is enablement (65%), an estimate; forecast-driven lines are 9% of cost. The tornado shows time_saved_parameter and realisation_rate move the result most. No public data links AI use to a company's outcomes, so value is a scenario projection, not a forecast. Uncertainty from the forecasts is widened by k = 1.5 and 2.0 because Phase 3 found its intervals too narrow.

## 9. Limitations

- Drivers are drawn independently; their correlations are unknown.
- Months beyond a driver's forecast horizon are extrapolated flat (section 7).
- BurstGPT is one provider's trace; only its relative variation is used as a demand factor.
- Tokens per active user comes from WildChat consumer chats and counts output tokens only; it is not an enterprise measurement.
- The time-saved scenarios come from survey (FRED GenAI tracker) and trial (METR) evidence; the downside applies METR's task-time increase to the illustrative share of AI-assisted hours.
- Phase 3 found the forecast intervals under-cover on the holdout; the multipliers 1.5 and 2.0 are a sensitivity response, not a calibration.
- Engine month 1 is the first forecast month of each driver; the drivers' data end at different dates.
- The example company's inputs are illustrative. The measured input-token share is 86%.

## 10. Deviations from the Phase 4 prompt

- There is no Epoch price forecast in Phase 3, so token_price uses the flat fixed_token_price_per_mtok input (flagged used_fallback); price_multiplier_vs_frontier is kept but has no effect.
- The prompt says input tokens are 83% of the blended price; the measured BurstGPT share in inputs_for_phase4.json is 86%. It is recorded only; there is no Epoch blended price to weight.
- The downside row is a task-time CHANGE (METR), not a share of work hours. It is converted with an extra illustrative input, ai_assisted_share_of_hours: fraction = -(value / 100) x that share.
- The token-demand run-rate covers month 1 only; months 2..T of token demand are flat-extrapolated.
- Extra input maintenance_during_build (bool) makes the 'during the build, if the user sets it' rule explicit.
- Sensitivity on 'base wages' scales staff and engineer wages together; adoption_target and realisation_rate are capped at 1.
- The data contract lists the forecast files from manifest.json and the engine role mapping with found/missing status.
